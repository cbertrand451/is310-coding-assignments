## Cultural Gender Differences Over Time


## Datasets

I used the Goodreads Top 500 Novels Dataset from [Responsible Datasets in Context Project](https://www.responsible-datasets-in-context.com/posts/top-500-novels/top-500-novels.html)

I also used the New York Times Bestseller Dataset from the [Post45 Data Collectove](https://data.post45.org/posts/nyt_hardcover_fiction_bestsellers/)

## Analysis 

This notebook explores how author gender appears across the Top 500 Novels dataset over time, using:
- publication decades
- author gender
- rank data

It also compares the Top 500 list with NYT bestseller records to see which books also had commercial bestseller visibility. 

## Questions Explored

- How does the share of male and female authors change by publication decade?
- Do author gender patterns differ across Top 500 ranking positions?
- Which Top 500 books also appeared on the NYT bestseller list?
- Does NYT bestseller visibility vary by author gender over time?


## Conclusion

The analysis finds that the dataset is male-dominated overall, but gender representation and bestseller presence vary by decade, so the results should be read as patterns in a curated literary fashion rather than all published novels.
